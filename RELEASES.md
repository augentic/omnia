## 0.39.0

Unreleased

### Added

- Committed guest bindings for every `omnia:*` interface in `omnia-sdk`,
  generated by `wit-bindgen` 0.63 from the interface crates' WIT.
  `mise run bindgen` regenerates them. The CI `bindings` job fails when
  they drift from the WIT, and checks with `wasm-tools` that a built guest
  targets the world they were generated from.
- One `omnia_sdk` module per interface, each in the same shape:
  - one opener, `open` or `connect`, taking a binding name as `&str`;
  - a `DEFAULT` where the interface has a default binding, and a
    `default()` that opens it;
  - the generated types a guest holds re-exported at the module root;
  - helpers as methods that take `&str` and `&[u8]` and return a
    `Result`, with an outcome the WIT names as the inner `Err` of a
    `Result<Result<T, Outcome>>`;
  - a short verb over borrowed arguments as the everyday form, beside the
    generated full-control name, and `_json` ending every helper that
    encodes or decodes JSON;
  - the generated interfaces as public submodules.
- The helpers, by module:
  - `keyvalue`: `Bucket::read`, `write`, `remove`, `contains`, `add`,
    `keys`, `get_json`, and `set_json`, and `Lifetime: From<Duration>`.
    The conditional writes go by the `Revision` an entry was read at:
    `compare_and_swap` for bytes, `get_json_entry` and `set_json_if` for
    JSON, and `update_json`, which retries its closure until a write lands.
  - `docstore`: `Collection::get_json`, `read`, `contains`, `insert`,
    `upsert`, `remove`, `find`, and `find_all`, `Document::decode`,
    `Query::builder`, `Filter::all` and `array_contains`, and
    `SortField::asc`, `desc`, and `compare`.
  - `blobstore`: `create` and `exists` for a container,
    `Container::read`, `read_range`, `write`, `write_with` for attributes,
    `metadata`, `contains`, and `remove`, and `Content::read`.
  - `sql`: `Database::begin` and `transaction`, which commits on `Ok` and
    rolls back on `Err`, and `query_as`, `query_opt`, and `exec` on a
    `Database` and a `Transaction` through the `Executor` trait, which is
    `Sync`, so a fn over `&impl Executor` stays `Send`. `params!`
    builds a statement's parameters, `FromRow` decodes a tuple, and
    `ResultSet::read` and `Statement::new` work over a `Value` whose cases
    take the WIT's names.
  - `http`: a `RequestBuilder` from `get`, `post`, `put`, `patch`, and
    `delete`, with `header`, `bearer`, `json`, `body`, and `timeout`, that
    ends in `build`, `fetch`, or `fetch_json`. `HttpCache::fetch_json`
    decodes a cached JSON reply.
  - `config`: `get`, `require`, and `parse`.
  - `identity`: `Identity::token`, which answers an `AccessToken` with its
    `expires_at` instant and `expires_in`, and `Identity::access_token`.
  - `messaging`: `Client::send`, `request`, and `reply`, and `Message::new`
    and `Message::json`, with `with_key`, `with_content_type`, and
    `with_metadata`.
  - `model`: `open`, and `Request::complete`, `complete_with`, and
    `complete_checked`, each answering `Result<Result<Reply, Exhausted>>`.
    On the generated records, `Message::user`, `assistant`, and `system`,
    `Format::schema_of`, `Tool::of`, `ToolCall::arguments`, and `Default`
    for `Generation`, `Format`, and `WorkerGrant`.
  - `plugins`: `load`, which answers `Result<Result<Plugin, Conflict>>`,
    and `Source::name`.
  - `vcs`: `open`, `init`, and `clone`, which answer a `Workspace`, and
    `Workspace::add`, `remove`, `fetch`, `remote_bookmark`, `push`,
    `log_all`, and `status_all`.
  - `vault`: `Locker::read`, `write`, `remove`, and `contains`, and
    `names`, which walks every page and answers each name once.
  - `websocket`: `Client::broadcast`, `send`, `close`, and `exists`.
- The unstable interfaces as raw bindings, with no helpers:
  `blobstore::uploads` and `presign`, `messaging::consumer`, `log`, and
  `admin`, and `otel::logs`.
- `From<types::Error> for omnia_sdk::Error`, so `?` on a binding call keeps
  the error's case as the code: a `BadGateway` for `unavailable`, and a
  `ServerError` for every other. An `anyhow` context around the call keeps
  the code too. Every interface's error converts by this one table.
- `From<Outcome> for omnia_sdk::Error` under the outcome's name, read from a
  topic or subcommand handler's source chain too. `model::Exhausted` is a
  `BadRequest`, and `plugins::Conflict`, `vcs::Occupied`, `vcs::Diverged`,
  and `vcs::Unmerged` are each a `Conflict`.
- `ErrorKind::Conflict`, a `409` that exits 5 and rejects a delivery, with
  `Error::conflict` and `conflict!`. `From<http::Error> for
  omnia_sdk::Error`, as a `ServerError` with the code `invalid`.
- Handlers that leave out the `Context`. A topic or subcommand handler is an
  `async fn(I)` or an `async fn(Context, I)`, and a closure of either shape.
- `Handlers::request` and `request_with`, which answer a request delivery.
  The reply is the handler's output as JSON, or the `Message` it returns,
  and goes to the delivery's `reply_to` through the `default` broker.
- `mcp::Tool::of`, a tool whose arguments are a `JsonSchema` type.
- `messaging::Delivery::reply_to`, `position`, `attempt`, and
  `published_at`, from the WIT delivery.
- `omnia_wasi_vcs::Error::Denied`, for a remote that refuses the caller.
- `omnia_sdk::exports::messaging` and `omnia_sdk::exports::websocket`. Each
  holds an `incoming-handler` `Guest` trait beside the `export!` that binds a
  hand-written implementation of it.
- `Option<T>` fields in `#[derive(Settings)]`. A field whose key is unset
  loads as `None`.
- Handler-test doubles in `omnia_test::host`, each served to the booted
  component:
  - `MatchedHttp` answers outbound requests by method and URL, through the
    new `Backends::http`.
  - `ScriptedTables` answers `omnia:sql` statements by predicate, through
    `Backends::sql`.
  - `Sink` records messages and WebSocket sends, through
    `Backends::messaging` and the new `Backends::websocket`.
  - `FixedIdentity` answers a fixed token for every identity, through
    `Backends::identity`.
- The `omnia_test::contract` rows `state::json_revision`,
  `state::update_json`, and `blob::attributes`. A backend's guest program
  calls them beside the other rows.
- `omnia_test::host::{send, deliver}`, which drive one request or one
  delivery through a booted runtime, and `precompiled`, which compiles a
  component once for every boot after the first.
- `omnia_sdk::http::cache`, behind the `http` feature. `HttpCache`
  answers an outbound request from an `omnia:keyvalue` bucket as its
  `Cache-Control` and `If-None-Match` ask. It replaces the
  `omnia-http-cache` crate of `omnia-ext`, with the same API, header
  contract, and store keys.
- The `omnia:*` WIT packages, published to the `omnia.host` wasm-pkg
  registry by the release workflow. Each release's description is the
  summary of its package docs, and `docs/reference/wit-packages.md` says
  how to fetch one. The tasks behind it:
  - `mise run wit-check` fails on a `wit/deps` copy that differs from
    the package that owns it, or a plain `//` comment, which wit-parser
    reads as docs. It parses every package with and without its
    `@unstable` features, and builds every release.
  - `mise run wit-verify` fails when a release the registry holds differs
    from the tree. CI's `WIT` job runs it beside `wit-check`.
  - `mise run wit-sync` rewrites every copy from the package that owns it.
- `Runtime::enter`, which instantiates a guest on a fresh store at a chain
  context. Every trigger and the command driver run a guest through it.
- `StoreCtx::guest` and `StoreBase::guest`, the guest a store runs.
- `#[derive(FromEnv)]`, from `omnia-core` and `omnia` under the trait's
  name. Each field of an options struct takes `#[env(from = "NAME")]`,
  with a `default` and a `with` parser where it needs them, a `default`
  alone for a field set in code, or `nested`. The struct takes
  `#[env(validate = path)]`, and `#[env(crate = omnia)]` where it reaches
  the derive through `omnia`. The derive emits:
  - `FromEnv`, which `Connect::connect` calls;
  - inherent `load_env`, `with_env`, which applies the variables that are
    set over options built in code, and `load_from`, which loads through a
    lookup in place of the process environment;
  - `Default`, where every field has a `default` or is an `Option`.
- `omnia_core::env`, re-exported as `omnia::env`, with the `list`,
  `millis`, and `paths` parsers a `with` names.
- `omnia::env::pattern` and `omnia::env::matches`. `pattern` checks a
  variable pattern, a whole name or a prefix that ends in `*`, and fails
  with a `PatternError`. `matches` applies one to a name.
- The host variables every guest sees, each a whole name or a prefix that
  ends in `*`:
  - `[environment] inherit` in the manifest, and `Manifest::inherit` with
    the `Environment` table;
  - `environment: { inherit: [..] }` in `runtime!`;
  - `--env NAME` on `run`, carried as `RunPlan::env`;
  - `omnia_test::host::Deployment::inherit`.
- `DeploymentBuilder::options` and `omnia_test::host::Deployment::options`
  set the `RuntimeOptions` a deployment runs under, in place of the
  environment's, before the engine and its instance pool are built.
- `RuntimeOptions::validate`, and `total_core_instances`,
  `total_memories`, `total_tables` and `total_stacks`, the pool totals a
  runtime builds.

### Changed

- A guest no longer sees the host's environment. Its `wasi:cli`
  environment holds `RUST_LOG`, `NO_COLOR` when the console is plain, and
  the host variables the deployment names. To keep a guest's variables,
  name them in `[environment] inherit` (`inherit = ["ORDERS_*"]`), in
  `runtime!` as `environment: { inherit: [..] }`, or with `run --env`.
  `inherit = ["*"]` passes every variable, as before. The variables
  `Metadata::from_env` reads, such as `CLI_REQUEST_ID`, arrive only when
  their prefix is passed, as `inherit = ["CLI_*"]` passes it.
- `omnia_sdk::api` is gone, and its items moved:
  - `Context`, `Metadata`, `Handler`, `Handlers`, and `Router` to the crate
    root;
  - `api::http` into `omnia_sdk::http`, beside the outbound `fetch` and
    `send`;
  - `api::messaging` into `omnia_sdk::messaging`, whose `export!` binds a
    router fn;
  - `api::command` to `omnia_sdk::command`.
- The handler types take no provider. `Context`, `Handlers`, and `Router`
  lose their type parameter, `Handler<I, M>` takes the fn's argument list
  as `M` in place of a provider, `Context` carries the call's `metadata`
  alone, and `Handlers::into_router()` replaces `with_state(provider)`.
- `omnia_sdk::Error` is a struct of an `ErrorKind`, a code, and a
  description, in place of an enum of four variants. A match on a variant
  reads `error.kind()` instead, and the constructors and macros keep their
  names. `Error` no longer implements `Serialize` or `Deserialize`, since
  `ErrorBody` is its wire form.
- `Router::handle` answers `Result<Option<Message>>`, a request route's
  reply, in place of `Result<()>`.
- `McpServer::call_tool` and `read_resource` are async, so a tool may await
  a capability. `mcp::router` and `handle_message` are generic over the
  server, and `handle_message` is async.
- `http::export!`, `messaging::export!`, and `command::export!` take the
  router fn alone. On a native target each binds nothing and still uses the
  fn, so a private router fn raises no `dead_code`.
- `model::prompt::Sections::channels` returns the generated `Message`.
- `Question::ask` takes no model, and answers `Result<Result<T, Exhausted>>`.
  An `Exhausted` carries the backend's own detail.
- The `model`, `vcs`, and `plugins` failures a guest sees are the binding
  error's case names:
  - model: `invalid` is a `500` `invalid`, no longer a `400`
    `invalid-request`, and `other` a `500` `other`, no longer a `502`
    `backend`. A spent budget is the `Exhausted` outcome.
  - vcs: a missing revision is `None`, no longer a `404`. A place with no
    workspace is a `500` `not-found`, no longer a `400`
    `not-a-repository`. An unreachable remote is `unavailable`, no longer
    `access`, and a place already taken is the `Occupied` outcome.
  - plugins: a refusal answers the host's case name at `500`, no longer
    `refused` or `internal`, and a missed pin is the `Conflict` outcome.
- A `check` reaches the guest as a `Candidate`, through
  `Request::complete_checked`, rather than as a tool call named `check`.
- `model`, `vcs`, `plugins`, and `docstore` re-export the generated records
  in place of the SDK's own, in the WIT's shape:
  - `model::Tool` is the WIT `tool` record, and `Format::Schema` holds a
    `Schema`, so `model::Function` and `SchemaFormat` go.
    `Generation::stop` is `stop_sequences`.
  - `vcs::Entry` is `LogEntry`, and `Rule::paths` is `pattern`. `Merged`
    and `CloneOptions` go, since `merge` and `clone` take and answer the
    WIT's own.
  - `plugins::Location` is `Source`, and `Plugin` is the WIT record, so
    `Digest` goes.
  - `docstore::SortField` is the WIT record, built by the same `asc` and
    `desc`.
- `sql::Value::Float`, `Double`, and `Str` are `Float32`, `Float64`, and
  `Text`. A zoned timestamp is a `TimestampTz`, so `Timestamp` binds
  `timestamp` whatever its text holds.
- `#[derive(Settings)]` reads through `omnia_sdk::config`, so `load()`
  takes no argument.
- The `omnia-wasi-*` crates and `omnia-plugin` are host-only.
  `omnia-plugin` is empty on `wasm32`, and the `omnia-wasi-*` crates are no
  longer built for it. Their guest halves moved into `omnia-sdk`:
  - the guest telemetry library is `omnia_sdk::otel`, with
    `#[omnia_sdk::otel::instrument]`;
  - `omnia_wasi_http::{serve, handle}` are `omnia_sdk::http::{serve, send}`;
  - `omnia_wasi_model::prompt` is `omnia_sdk::model::prompt`.
- `omnia_sdk::wasi::messaging::handle` is `omnia_sdk::messaging::handle`,
  and `omnia_sdk::wasi::command::execute` is `omnia_sdk::command::execute`.
- `omnia-sdk` depends on `wasip3` alone. Its bindings run on the
  `wit-bindgen` runtime `wasip3` re-exports.
- The `omnia_test::contract` rows run in a guest over the bindings, and take
  the bindings' own handles. The `document` rows run over `DocStoreDefault`
  in place of `MemoryDocs`.
- Every `omnia` build carries the `run` grammar. `omnia::Cli`,
  `omnia::Command`, and `omnia::Parser` are always exported, and a
  direct-command binary links `clap`.
- `omnia::Command::Compile` is a variant in every build, not only with
  `jit`. The generated `main` refuses it, as before.
- Every `omnia:*` host reports one `omnia_wasi_types::Error`.
  `omnia-wasi-types` binds `omnia:types` for the host, and each host crate
  maps its vendored copy onto that binding. Each crate's `Error` re-exports
  it, except in `omnia-wasi-vcs`, whose backends keep an `Error` of their
  own. A typed error a backend returns keeps its kind whichever host carries
  it, where another host's `Error` reached the guest as `other`.
- `omnia_wasi_blobstore::Error::missing_container` is the free fn
  `omnia_wasi_blobstore::missing_container`.
- `DocStoreClient::query` takes the WIT's `Filter` in place of an
  `Option<FilterTree>`, and `omnia-wasi-docstore` re-exports the generated
  `Condition`, `Filter`, `SortField`, `Test`, and `Value`:
  - every group must hold, and any condition in a group;
  - `negated` flips a condition's test;
  - an empty filter selects every document.

  An `array-contains` test reaches the backend, and `DocStoreDefault` fails
  it with `unsupported`, as the host did before.
- `omnia_wasi_docstore::QueryOpts` holds `order_by`, `limit`, and
  `continuation`. Its `limit` is a `u32` from 1 to 1,000 that the host
  always sets.
- `Endpoint::request` takes a `timeout: Option<Duration>` in place of an
  `Option<RequestOptions>`. The host never sends a zero `timeout`.
- `StoreConfig` takes the `guest` the store is built for, and an `env` of
  `None` gives the guest an empty environment, where it inherited the
  host's.
- `RuntimeParts` takes `inherit`, the host variables guests see. Empty, a
  guest sees only `RUST_LOG`, and `NO_COLOR` when the console is plain.
- `RuntimeOptions` and the `ConnectOptions` of `omnia-wasi-http`,
  `omnia-wasi-keyvalue`, `omnia-wasi-vault`, `omnia-wasi-model`,
  `omnia-wasi-docstore`, and `omnia-wasi-identity` derive omnia's
  `FromEnv`:
  - a load fails with one error naming every variable that is unset or
    does not parse, and no error prints a value;
  - a value that is not valid Unicode is invalid, where it read as unset;
  - `RuntimeOptions` and `omnia_wasi_http::ConnectOptions` gain `Default`.
- `POOL_TOTAL_CORE_INSTANCES` and `POOL_TOTAL_TABLES` default to four
  times `POOL_MAX_INSTANCES`, `POOL_TOTAL_STACKS` to twice it, and
  `POOL_TOTAL_MEMORIES` to `POOL_MAX_INSTANCES`, where each was a flat
  `1000`. A guest holds three core instances and two tables, so the flat
  totals filled near a third of `POOL_MAX_INSTANCES`. It takes a second
  stack while it blocks in a call and the host completes another. The
  `RuntimeOptions` fields are `Option<u32>`, `None` when unset.
- The table and stack pools map more at the defaults: about 625 MiB of
  tables, up from about 160 MiB, and 4 GiB of stacks, up from 2 GiB. A
  page is backed only once it is touched, so only a host with strict
  overcommit is charged for the whole mapping.
- A deployment validates its runtime options once
  `DeploymentBuilder::guest_timeout` and `max_dispatch_depth` apply, so
  `guest_timeout(Duration::ZERO)` fails the build where it was accepted.

### Removed

- The capability traits: `BlobStore`, `Config`, `DocumentDb`, `HttpClient`,
  `Identity`, `Model`, `Plugins`, `Publisher`, `SecretStore`, `SqlDb`,
  `StateStore`, `Vcs`, and `WebSockets`, with their `*Ext` and `Has*`
  traits and `omnia_sdk::Page`. A guest calls the bindings.
- `omnia_sdk::WasiProvider` and `omnia_sdk::wasi`.
- `model::Error`, `vcs::Error`, and `plugins::Error`. A call fails with the
  binding error, or answers a typed outcome.
- `model::CHECK_TOOL` and `model::Request::check`.
- The per-path `vcs` fns, such as `resolve`, `head`, `commit`, and
  `labelled`. A guest calls the generated `Workspace` methods.
- `docstore::Query::offset` and `docstore::ScalarValue::Binary`. The wire
  carries neither.
- `#[derive(Provider)]` and `#[omnia_sdk::capability]`.
- `Context::provider` and the `Context` accessors, such as `state` and
  `sql`.
- The guest bindings of the `omnia-wasi-*` crates and `omnia-plugin`:
  `omnia_wasi_*::omnia`, `omnia_plugin::omnia`, and the `omnia-wasi-types`
  guest module.
- The `guest` feature of `omnia-test`, and `omnia_test::guest` with it:
  `Provider`, `Memory`, `MemoryDocs`, `MapConfig`, `ScriptedLoader`,
  `ScriptedVcs`, and the native doubles. A handler test boots its component
  over `omnia_test::host`.
- Six features. A manifest that names one no longer resolves:
  - `cli`, `mpk`, and `gc` on `omnia`;
  - `mpk` and `gc` on `omnia-core`;
  - `jit` on `omnia-cli`.
- `RuntimeOptions::pool_total_gc_heaps`, `pool_memory_protection_keys`, and
  `pool_max_memory_protection_keys`. The runtime no longer reads
  `POOL_TOTAL_GC_HEAPS`, `POOL_MEMORY_PROTECTION_KEYS`, or
  `POOL_MAX_MEMORY_PROTECTION_KEYS`, and ignores them when set.
- `host_error!`, from `omnia-core` and `omnia`. No host defines an `Error`
  of its own.
- `omnia_wasi_docstore::{FilterTree, ScalarValue, ComparisonOp}`, the
  hand-written `omnia_wasi_docstore::SortField`, and `QueryOpts::offset`. A
  backend reads the generated `Filter` and `SortField`.
- `Container::name`, `ContainerMetadata::name`, and
  `ObjectMetadata::container` from `omnia-wasi-blobstore`. The host keeps
  each container's name on its handle.
- `omnia_wasi_messaging::RequestOptions`, whose `expected_replies` the host
  never set.
- `Runtime::store`, `store_in`, `build_store`, `instantiate`, and
  `store_factory`. A trigger calls `Runtime::enter`, and reads a client
  through `Provides::borrow` on a clone of `Runtime::backends`.
- The `fromenv` builder of `RuntimeOptions` and the `omnia-wasi-*`
  `ConnectOptions`: `from_env()`, the `*Builder` it returned with a setter
  per field and `finalize()`, and `requirements()`. `with_env` applies the
  environment over options built in code, and `load_from` loads through a
  lookup.
- The `fromenv` dependency, which the `oauth` feature of
  `omnia-wasi-identity` no longer enables.

## 0.38.0

Unreleased

### Added

- `omnia_sdk::WasiProvider`: one zero-sized provider that implements every
  capability trait over the WASI bindings on `wasm32`. It replaces a guest's
  provider struct with one empty impl per capability, and the `WasiModel`,
  `WasiVcs`, and `WasiPlugins` handles.
- `omnia_sdk::wasi::{identity, keyvalue, sql, vault}::Handle`, on `wasm32`:
  the handle `named(name)` answers on `WasiProvider`.
- `#[derive(Provider)]`, `#[derive(Settings)]`, and `#[derive(FromRow)]`,
  re-exported by `omnia_sdk`:
  - `Provider` composes a provider from one field per capability. A field
    names an `omnia_sdk` capability bare, as in `#[provider(Config)]`, and
    any other by path, as in `#[provider(crate::geo::Geocoder)]`.
  - `Settings` loads a part's required configuration in one call.
  - `FromRow` decodes a SQL row into a record type.
- `#[omnia_sdk::capability]`, which makes a trait declared outside
  `omnia_sdk` a capability a `Provider` field delegates.
- `omnia_sdk::api::{Handlers, Router}`: a module registers its HTTP routes
  (`route`), messaging topics (`topic`, `topic_with`), and CLI subcommands
  (`command`, `command_with`, behind the `command` feature) on one
  `Handlers<P>`, and `with_state(provider)` closes them into the one
  `Router<P>`. `http::export!`, `messaging::export!`, and
  `command::export!` bind the WASI exports. `command::export!` stamps the
  guest crate's version and description as `--version` and `--help`.
- `SecretStore`, the secrets capability over `omnia:vault`. A handle
  addresses one locker, `default` until `named(name)` selects another, and
  `keys(after)` lists it a page at a time, in no particular order.
- One `Context` accessor per capability: `blobs`, `config`, `docs`, `http`,
  `identity`, `model`, `plugins`, `publisher`, `secrets`, `sql`, `state`,
  `vcs`, and `websockets`. Each borrows the provider as that one trait, so
  a handler bounded on `Config + StateStore` calls `get` without UFCS.
- `omnia:types@0.1.0`, the types every `omnia:*` package shares, and
  `omnia-wasi-types`, the crate that holds it:
  - `error`, why a capability call failed, with one case per capability
    error code. Each package's docs say what each case means for it.
  - `timestamp`, an instant in milliseconds since the Unix epoch. Every
    package gives its instants as one, except `omnia:otel`, which keeps
    OTLP's nanoseconds.
  - `json`, a JSON document as UTF-8 text.

  Each package vendors the WIT. On `wasm32`, every `omnia-wasi-*` binding
  maps it onto `omnia_wasi_types::omnia::types`, so a guest matches one
  `Error` whichever capability returned it.
- `omnia:keyvalue`, the Omnia-owned interface `StateStore` calls.
  `WasiKeyValue` serves it over one `Bucket`:
  - `store.open` answers a `bucket` handle, and every call goes through
    it.
  - A write names its `lifetime`: `forever`, a TTL in milliseconds, or one
    of two that leave an existing entry the time it has left.
  - A write returns the entry's `revision`. `set` takes an `absent` or
    `matches` precondition, and `delete` a revision. One that fails
    answers a `conflict` carrying the entry it found.
  - `increment` takes a TTL in milliseconds, which applies only to a
    counter it creates.
  - `get-many`, `set-many`, and `delete-many` take several keys at once.
  - The optional `keys` interface pages the keys under a prefix.
  - `WasiKeyValue` answers `unsupported` for nothing.
- `StateStore::get_entry`, which reads a value with its `Revision`, and
  `StateStore::set_if`, which writes while a `Precondition` holds. One
  that does not hold changes nothing and answers the entry it found. An
  implementor provides both, and `compare_and_swap` is provided over them.
- `omnia:sql`, the Omnia-owned interface `SqlDb` calls. `WasiSql` serves it
  over one `Connection`:
  - `store.open` answers a `database` handle, and every statement goes
    through it.
  - `query` answers a `result-set`: the `columns` it selects, a `rows`
    stream, and a `finished` future.
  - `execute` answers the rows a statement changed, and `batch` runs its
    statements in one transaction.
  - A temporal value is text in one ISO 8601 shape per case, and the host
    fails any other with `invalid`.
  - The optional `transactions` interface begins a `transaction` on a
    database. Its statements commit or roll back together, and a failure
    or a drop rolls it back.
- `SqlDb::begin`, which starts an `omnia_sdk::sql::Transaction`. Its
  `query`, `execute`, and `query_as` run in the transaction until `commit`
  or `rollback` ends it. Dropping it rolls it back, and so does a statement
  that fails. `omnia_sdk::wasi::sql::Transaction` is the one `WasiProvider`
  begins.
- `omnia:blobstore`, the Omnia-owned interface `BlobStore` calls.
  `WasiBlobStore` serves it over one `BlobStoreClient`:
  - `store.open` answers a `container` handle, and every object call goes
    through it.

### Changed

- omnia runs WASI 0.3 guests alone. Guests build for `wasm32-wasip3`, and
  the MSRV is Rust 1.100. The linker no longer serves WASI 0.2, so a guest
  that imports a WASI 0.2 `wasi:cli`, `wasi:clocks`, `wasi:filesystem`,
  `wasi:http`, `wasi:io`, `wasi:random`, or `wasi:sockets` interface is
  refused by name, with the rebuild it needs, at startup or at its first
  use.
- `omnia_test::build` compiles a fixture crate's programs for
  `wasm32-wasip3`.
- A raw export that may wait is an `async func`. On `wasm32-wasip3`,
  `std::thread::sleep` and every async-typed import suspend the task, and
  a sync-typed export that suspends traps with `cannot block a synchronous
  task before returning`. The exports behind the `export!` macros are
  already async-typed.
- A handler takes its `Context<P>` first and its input second. An HTTP
  handler is a plain axum handler that takes `Context<P>` as an extractor
  over the `Arc<P>` in the router state. A handler that takes `Json<T>`
  answers `415` to a request without `content-type: application/json`.
- `omnia_sdk::api::Client` is gone, and the provider is the router's state:
  - `with_state(Client::new(provider))` becomes `with_state(provider)`.
  - A raw axum router closes with `with_state(Arc::new(provider))`.
  - `Command::new` takes the `&Arc<P>` where it took a `&Client<P>`.
- `omnia_sdk::Error` implements `IntoResponse`, so a handler answers
  `omnia_sdk::Result<T>`. `HttpResult` and `HttpError` are gone.
- `omnia_sdk::command!(main)` is `omnia_sdk::api::command::export!(main)`.
- The fns a hand-written WASI export calls live in `omnia_sdk::wasi`, on
  `wasm32`:
  - `wasi::http::serve`, which was `api::http::serve`. It takes any router
    that converts into an `axum::Router`, opens the guest telemetry scope,
    and layers `api::http::trace`, so the export does neither itself.
  - `wasi::messaging::handle`, which was `api::messaging::handle`. It opens
    the guest telemetry scope.
  - `wasi::command::execute`, which was `api::command::execute_wasi`.
- The value types live in the SDK's own modules:
  - `omnia_sdk::docstore`, which was `omnia_sdk::document_store`.
  - `omnia_sdk::sql` (`Value`, `Row`, `Field`, `Statement`, `FromRow`).
    `omnia_wasi_sql` exported `Row` and `Field`, and `DataType` is `Value`.
  - `omnia_sdk::messaging::Message`, which was `omnia_sdk::Message`.
  - `omnia_sdk::blobstore::{ObjectMetadata, ContainerMetadata}` and
    `omnia_sdk::identity::AccessToken`.
  - A `DEFAULT` binding name in `omnia_sdk::{identity, keyvalue, sql,
    vault}`, and `omnia_sdk::keyvalue::Conflict`.
- Every capability trait but `Vcs`, `Plugins`, and `Model` answers
  `omnia_sdk::Result`. Those three answer their module's typed `Error`,
  which converts into an `omnia_sdk::Error` under its `code()`. A trait
  over named bindings (`Identity`, `SecretStore`, `SqlDb`, and
  `StateStore`) addresses `default` until `named(name)` selects another
  name the deployment declares. Migrating a 0.37 guest:
  - `Config::get` answers `Option<String>`, so an unset key is `Ok(None)`.
    `ConfigExt::require` fails one with the code `not-found`, and
    `ConfigExt::parse` answers it as `None`.
  - `HttpRequest` is `HttpClient`, and `fetch` takes a `Request<Bytes>`.
    `fetch_json` takes the output type alone.
  - `Identity::access_token(identity)` is `named(identity)` and then
    `token(scopes)`, which answers an `AccessToken`. Its `expires_in` is a
    `Duration`, and its `Debug` redacts the token.
    `IdentityExt::access_token()` answers the token alone.
  - `Publish::send` is `Publisher::publish`. A `Message` payload is
    `Bytes`, and `Message::new` takes `&[u8]`.
  - `Broadcast::send(name, data, sockets)` is `WebSockets::broadcast(data)`
    to every socket, or `WebSockets::send_to(sockets, data)`.
  - `TableStore` is `SqlDb`. `query` and `execute`, which was `exec`, take
    the SQL and its `&[Value]` on the handle, not a connection name.
    `execute` answers a `u64`, and `batch` runs `Statement`s.
  - `DocumentStore` is `DocumentDb`. `query` takes `&Query`, which was
    `QueryOptions` with `after` for `continuation`, and answers a
    `Page<Document>`. A query that sets `offset` fails with the code
    `unsupported`, so a guest pages with `after` and `Page::next`.
    `insert` answers `Ok(Err(Exists))` for an id already stored, and a
    `Document` payload is `Bytes`.
  - `BlobStore` answers `None` for a missing object. A call on an object
    in a container that does not exist fails with the code `not-found`,
    and `delete_container` succeeds when the container is absent.
    `get_range` takes a half-open `Range<u64>` clamped to the object, and
    `list(container, prefix, after)` answers a `Page<ObjectMetadata>`.
    `object_info` is `stat`. The `BlobStoreExt` methods `has`,
    `delete_objects`, `copy_object`, `move_object`, and `clear` are the
    provided `exists`, `delete_many`, `copy`, `rename`, and `clear`.
  - `ObjectMetadata`, `ContainerMetadata`, `AccessToken`, `Document`,
    `Message`, `Delivery`, and `Statement` are `#[non_exhaustive]`, so
    code outside the SDK builds them with `new`. An
    `ObjectMetadata::created_at` is a `SystemTime`, and a
    `ContainerMetadata::created_at` is an `Option<SystemTime>`, `None`
    where the store records no time.
- `SqlDb` calls `omnia:sql`, opening a `database` handle for each call, and
  `batch` runs its statements in one transaction. A failing statement rolls
  back the whole batch.
- Every `SqlDb` implementor provides `begin` and its `Transaction` type.
- `omnia_sdk::sql::Value` mirrors `omnia:sql`, where `DataType` wrapped
  each case in an `Option`:
  - `Value::Null` is a `NULL` of any type, and a `NULL` reads back as it.
  - Every other case holds its value bare, as in `Value::Int64(1)`.
    `From` builds one from a value, or from an `Option` whose `None` is
    `Null`.
  - `Uint32` and `Uint64` are gone. A `u32` converts into an `Int64`, and
    a `u64` binds once the caller converts it to an `i64`.
  - `Binary` holds `Bytes`.
  - A `Timestamp` with a `Z` or an offset travels as a `timestamp-tz`.
- `BlobStore` calls `omnia:blobstore`, opening a `container` handle for
  each object call. Its codes come from the error's case, where the SDK
  read a missing container or object from the message.
- `SqlDefault` opens only the names it declares, and refuses any other.
  A guest that opened a connection by another name, such as `db`, opens
  `default` or lists the name in `SQL_DATABASES`.
- `SqlClient::open` fails a name the deployment does not declare with
  `omnia_wasi_sql::Error::NotFound`.
- `omnia_wasi_sql::Connection` serves `omnia:sql` alone:
  - `query` and `execute`, which was `exec`, take a `Vec<Value>`, and
    `execute` answers a `u64`.
  - `batch` runs a `Vec<Statement>` in one transaction. A `Statement` is
    its `sql` and `params`.
  - A `Row` has no `index`.
  - A backend picks a failure's code by returning an
    `omnia_wasi_sql::Error`, whose six cases are the six capability error
    codes.
  - `select` answers a `ResultSet`, the selected columns beside the rows.
    Its default body runs `query` and names the first row's columns.
  - `begin`, `commit`, and `rollback` bound the transaction the host runs
    on the connection. Their default bodies send `BEGIN`, `COMMIT`, and
    `ROLLBACK` through `execute`.
- `WasiSql` fails a statement that starts or ends a transaction or a
  savepoint, such as `BEGIN`, `COMMIT`, or `SAVEPOINT`, with the code
  `invalid`. A guest ends a transaction through its handle, and
  `SET TRANSACTION` still reaches the backend.
- `SqlDefault` serves transactions. Every handle on a database shares its
  one `SQLite` connection, so a call made while a transaction is open waits
  up to 5 s for it and then fails with the code `unavailable`, as a busy or
  locked database does. A DDL statement answers `0` rows changed.
- `SqlDefault` stores a `date` or `time` as the text it is given. It stored
  one as `NULL` before.
- `Row::get` reads a `bool` from an integer `0` or `1` too, which is how
  SQLite returns a `BOOLEAN`.
- Collection names are strict. `DocStoreDefault` opens only the
  collections it declares, any other fails with `not-found`, and a write
  no longer creates one. A deployment whose guests use a collection other
  than `default` lists it in `DOCSTORE_COLLECTIONS`.
- `DocStoreClient::insert` answers `false` for an id already stored, and a
  backend picks a failure's code by returning an
  `omnia_wasi_docstore::Error`.
- `omnia_wasi_docstore::{Document, ScalarValue, ComparisonOp, SortField,
  QueryResult}` are the crate's own types, where the WIT generated them.
  Their names and shapes are unchanged, so a backend builds as before.
- A `Timestamp` filter value reaches a backend as `ScalarValue::Str`, since
  `omnia:docstore` carries it as text. `omnia-azure-table` therefore
  compares it as a string, not as an `Edm.DateTime`.
- `DocumentDb::query` sends a filter as an `and` of `or`s. It pushes each
  `not` down to the fields and distributes each `or` over the `and`s inside
  it.
- `DocumentDb::query` fails a filter past its limits with the code
  `invalid` before the query reaches the host, where the guest trapped
  before:
  - A filter nests at most 5 deep, a list holds 1 to 100 values, and an
    `and` or `or` holds a child.
  - A filter holds at most 100 conditions once written as an `and` of
    `or`s.
  - A `Binary` value, a `Float64` that is not finite, and an ordering
    comparison with `null` or a boolean are refused.

  `MemoryDocs` refuses the same filters, and the host refuses a filter past
  its own limits with `invalid` too.
- `MemoryDocs` fails a query that sets `offset` with the code
  `unsupported`, and a write whose body is not a JSON object with
  `invalid`, as the runtime does.
- `IdentityStub` refuses an identity `IDENTITY_NAMES` does not list, and
  `IdentityDefault` serves the identity `default` alone. A refused name is
  `not-found`. A backend picks a failure's code by returning an
  `omnia_wasi_identity::Error`, whose six cases are the six capability
  error codes.
- `omnia_wasi_identity::AccessToken` is the crate's own struct, which the
  host maps onto the `omnia:identity` record. Its `expires_in` is the whole
  seconds the token has left when the backend answers, and its `Debug`
  redacts the token. `IdentityDefault` answers a cached token with the time
  it has left, where it answered the lifetime the token was issued with.
- Model names are strict. `ModelDefault` opens only the models it
  declares, and any other fails with `not-found`. A deployment whose guests
  open a model other than `default` lists it in `MODEL_NAMES`. What an
  echo cannot honour is `unsupported`: a `format::schema` and a set
  `generation` field.
- A model backend picks a failure's code by returning an
  `omnia_wasi_model::Error`, whose six cases are the six capability error
  codes. `Error::InvalidRequest` is `Error::Invalid`, and a spent budget is
  an `omnia_wasi_model::Exhausted`, where it was `Error::BudgetExhausted`.
  `ToolFailed` and `Backend` give way to the case that names the failure.
- `omnia_wasi_model::{Request, Generation, Tool, Function, Usage}` are the
  crate's own types, where the WIT generated them. A backend changes where
  their shapes do:
  - `Request::model` is the name the guest opened the model by, `default`
    included, where it was an optional hint the guest passed through. A
    backend maps `default` onto its own default model.
  - `Generation::effort` is an `Effort`, which loses `Minimal` and gains
    `Other`, a level by the backend's own name. `Effort::as_str` names the
    level, and a backend fails one it does not know with
    `Error::Invalid`.
  - `Function::description` is an `Option`.
  - `Tool` has the one variant `Function`.
  - `Usage` counts in `u64`, and adds `cache_read_tokens` and
    `cache_write_tokens`.
- `omnia_wasi_model::WorkspaceGrant` holds the lent `Location`, which has
  the fields the grant had, and the `worker` grant that `Grants` held
  beside it. `Grants::location` and `Grants::worker` read them.
- `WasiProvider`'s `Model` maps each `omnia:model` outcome onto the
  `model::Error` variant that names it:
  - `invalid` is `InvalidRequest`. A tool result or correction over the
    host's size cap is one, where it was `ToolFailed`.
  - `exhausted` is `BudgetExhausted`. Its detail is the last correction
    when the last candidate was rejected, and the `exhausted` detail
    otherwise.
  - `not-found`, `denied`, `unsupported`, and `unavailable` are the new
    `NotFound`, `Denied`, `Unsupported`, and `Unavailable`.
  - `other` is `Backend`.

  A declared tool named `check` fails with `InvalidRequest`, because a
  check reaches the handler under that name.
- `omnia_sdk::model::Usage` counts in `u64`, and adds `cache_read_tokens`
  and `cache_write_tokens`.
- `omnia_sdk::model::Effort` is no longer `Copy`, since `Effort::Other`
  holds a name.
- `omnia_wasi_config::ConfigClient` serves `omnia:config`:
  - `get` answers the value set for one key, or `None` when it is unset.
    It replaces `get_config`, which lent the whole map as a
    `WasiConfigVariables`.
  - `entries` answers every entry a guest may read. Its default body fails
    with `omnia_wasi_config::Error::Unsupported`.
  - A backend picks a failure's code by returning an
    `omnia_wasi_config::Error`, whose six cases are the six capability
    error codes.
- `ConfigDefault::connect` skips a variable whose name or value is not
  Unicode, where it panicked. `Default` builds an empty one, and
  `FromIterator` builds one from key-value pairs.
- `omnia::LoadError` has six cases, the six capability error codes, which
  were `Refused`, `Unavailable`, and `Internal`. `GuestError` and
  `AcquireError` name the same classes where they had `Refused`:
  - a `GuestError::Conflict` carries the digest of the bytes it turned
    away;
  - a `GuestError` for a missing file is `NotFound`, which was
    `Unavailable`;
  - a `GuestError` for a runtime with no registry client is
    `Unsupported`, which was `Internal`.
- `omnia::Plugins::load` and `PluginLoader::load` answer
  `Result<Result<Plugin, LoadConflict>, LoadError>`. An
  `omnia::LoadConflict` is a load that loaded nothing because its bytes
  missed its pin or its name is active under other bytes. It carries the
  digest found and the `Plugin` active under the name.
- `WasiProvider`'s `Plugins` folds the six cases onto its three codes, and
  answers a conflict as `refused`. Four loads change code:
  - a missing component file is `refused`, which was `unavailable`;
  - a path the filesystem refuses to read, such as a link that leads out
    of its mount, is `refused`, which was `unavailable`;
  - a registry digest that is not `sha256` is `internal`, which was
    `refused`;
  - a registry's credential, metadata, or content failure is
    `unavailable`, which was `refused`.
- `BlobStoreClient` states the container rules every backend keeps:
  - `get_container` fails a container that does not exist with
    `omnia_wasi_blobstore::Error::missing_container`.
  - `create_container` opens a container that already exists.
  - `delete_container` succeeds when the container is absent.

  A backend picks a failure's code by returning an
  `omnia_wasi_blobstore::Error`, whose six cases are the six capability
  error codes.
- `omnia_wasi_blobstore::Container` changes shape:
  - `get_data` takes a half-open `Range<u64>`, clamped to the object. An
    inverted range, which a backend refused, reads as empty.
  - `object_info` answers `None` for an absent object.
  - `list_objects(prefix, after)` answers one name-ordered `Page` of
    `ObjectMetadata`.

  `ContainerMetadata`, `ObjectMetadata`, and `Page` are the crate's own
  structs, which the host maps onto the `omnia:blobstore` records. The
  host resolves a `byte-range` to a `Range<u64>`, and folds and limits a
  listing itself.
- `StateStore` calls `omnia:keyvalue`, opening a `bucket` handle for each
  call:
  - `get` answers `Option<Bytes>`.
  - `set` answers `()`, and a TTL is an `Option<Duration>`. A zero TTL
    fails with the code `invalid` and writes nothing.
  - `cas` is `compare_and_swap`. It writes under the `absent`
    precondition, or under the `revision` it read the expected value at.
    A mismatch answers `Ok(Err(Conflict))` with the value it found.
  - `increment` takes a TTL. Like `compare_and_swap`, it applies the TTL
    only to an entry it creates.
  - `named(name)` selects another bucket the deployment declares.
  - A portable key works on every backend: it holds only ASCII letters,
    digits, `.`, `_`, and `-`, and neither starts nor ends with `.`. A
    backend may reject any other key with the code `invalid`.
- `StateStoreExt::{get_json, set_json}` fail a value that does not encode
  or decode with the code `invalid`.
- `StateStore` opens the `default` bucket, which was `cache`. The host
  stores a guest's bytes as they are and expires them itself, so a value a
  0.37 guest set with a TTL reads back as its JSON `Cacheable` envelope.
  `omnia_test::host::Backends::state` reads the raw bytes.
- Bucket names are strict. `KeyValueDefault` opens only the buckets it
  declares, and any other fails with `not-found`. A deployment whose guests
  open a bucket other than `default` lists it in `KEYVALUE_BUCKETS`.
- Locker names are strict. `VaultDefault` opens only the lockers it
  declares, and any other fails with `not-found`. A deployment whose guests
  open a locker other than `default` lists it in `VAULT_LOCKERS`. A backend
  picks a failure's code by returning an `omnia_wasi_vault::Error`, whose
  six cases are the six capability error codes.
- `Bucket` carries the revisions `omnia:keyvalue` speaks:
  - `get` answers an `Entry`, the stored bytes and their `Revision`.
  - `set` takes a `Lifetime` and an optional `Precondition`, and answers
    the revision it wrote. A precondition that does not hold answers the
    entry found.
  - `delete` takes an optional revision, and answers the entry found when
    it does not match.
  - `increment` takes the TTL of a counter it creates.
  - `swap` is gone, and a `set` with a precondition replaces it.

  A backend that keeps no revision of its own reports `Revision::of` the
  stored bytes. A backend picks a failure's code by returning an
  `omnia_wasi_keyvalue::Error`.
- `KeyValueDefault` and `omnia_test::guest::Memory` store a counter as
  decimal text, as `omnia:keyvalue` specifies, rather than as eight
  big-endian bytes. `increment` fails any other value with the code
  `invalid`.
- Each `Has<Trait>` names the type it delegates to as `Inner`, and its
  accessor returns `&Self::Inner`, so a capability's associated types
  forward to the field's. A `pub` provider that derives `Provider` needs
  `pub` field types.
- `omnia_test::guest::Scripted` is `ScriptedModel`, and
  `omnia_test::guest::Memory` implements `SecretStore`.
- `omnia_test::guest::Memory` keeps state per bucket and secrets per
  locker. `Memory::bucket` and `Memory::locker` declare a bucket and a
  locker beside `default`, `Memory::named` addresses another bucket and
  locker of the same store, and `Memory::snapshot` answers state by bucket.
- `omnia_test::guest::Memory` fails a call on an object in a container
  nothing created with the code `not-found`, as the runtime does. A test
  creates the container first, or seeds it with `insert_container` or
  `insert_object`.
- The `omnia_test::guest` doubles follow the traits:
  - `Provider`'s `publish`, `broadcast`, and `tables` fields and builders
    are `publisher`, `websockets`, and `sql`.
  - `ScriptedTables::statements` answers `Executed`, which was
    `Statement`, and its `binding` was `connection`. `on_exec` takes a
    `u64`.
  - `ScriptedTables::begin` answers a `ScriptedTransaction`, whose
    statements the same rules answer. `ScriptedTables::transactions` lists
    each one begun, with its statements and how it `Ended`.
  - `Broadcasted` has no channel `name`.
  - `MapConfig` answers an unknown key as unset.
  - `FixedIdentity` answers a named identity's token as `<token>:<name>`.
- `WasiHttp` is its own `HostCtx` carrier, like every other host. A bundle
  implements `Provides<WasiHttp>`, and `HttpBorrow` is
  `omnia_wasi_http::HttpBorrow`. `runtime!` no longer matches a `hosts:`
  row by the name `WasiHttp`, so an aliased row links.
- `Host<B>` is over the backend bundle, no longer the store type, and
  carries `run`. `add_to_linker` takes a `&mut Linker<StoreCtx<B>>`, and
  `run(&Runtime<B>)` resolves at once unless a trigger host (`WasiHttp`,
  `WasiMessaging`, `WasiWebSocket`) overrides it with its serve loop. A
  host implements it once, as `impl<B: Provides<Self>> Host<B>`.
- `omnia::Backends`, the trait the generated bundle implements to connect
  every backend, is `omnia::ConnectAll`, and `ConnectAll: Backends`.
- `Deployment<B>`, `DeploymentBuilder::build::<B>()`,
  `Deployment::host::<H>()`, and `Wiring::link(&mut Deployment<B>)` take
  the bundle alone. `build::<StoreCtx<Backends>>()` is
  `build::<Backends>()`, and `host::<WasiOtel, Backends>()` is
  `host::<WasiOtel>()`.
- `Registry<B>`, `RegistryParts<B>`, `Guest<B>`, `LinkSeam<B>`,
  `StoreFactory<B>`, and `FreshCall<B>` are over the bundle, each fixing
  the store to `StoreCtx<B>`. `Runtime::registry` and `Runtime::guest`
  return `&Registry<B>` and `Arc<Guest<B>>`.
- `runtime!` names each backend field by its row's position, so two
  backends whose paths end in the same name (`keyvalue::Client`,
  `model::Client`) no longer share a field.
- The HTTP trigger serves HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2 on one listener. On stop it
  accepts no new connection and lets each open one finish its requests.
- A server-mode deployment drains on the first `SIGINT` or `SIGTERM`, and
  exits on the second.
- Without a `Forwarded` header, the HTTP trigger takes a request's scheme
  and authority from its URI before the `Host` header, so an HTTP/2
  request needs no `Host`.
- Guests link `wasip3` 0.10 and `wit-bindgen` 0.63. `omnia:model` and
  `omnia:vcs` use `wasi:filesystem` and `wasi:clocks` at 0.3.2.
- `omnia::Backend` is `omnia::Connect`.
- Each host's client trait is named for its interface: `Wasi<Name>Ctx` is
  `<Name>Client`, as in `KeyValueClient`, `BlobStoreClient`, and
  `ModelClient`.
- `WasiBlobstore` and `BlobstoreDefault` are `WasiBlobStore` and
  `BlobStoreDefault`. `omnia::WasiPluginsCtxView` is
  `omnia::WasiPluginsView`.
- The messaging and websocket resource trait `Client` is `Endpoint`, and
  `MessagingClient::endpoint` and `WebSocketClient::endpoint` replace
  `connect`. `ClientProxy` keeps its name.
- `omnia_wasi_messaging::HandleError` follows how the guest settled the
  delivery. `Rejected` carries the guest's reason, where it carried an
  error. `Released` is a delivery the guest gave back, with the least delay
  it asked for. A backend that redelivers delivers a released message
  again, and drops or dead-letters a rejected one.
- The document store's WIT package is `omnia:docstore`, which was
  `wasi:docstore`, so a guest built against `wasi:docstore` is rebuilt.
- The packages 0.37 shipped are rewritten, so a 0.37 guest that imports
  one is rebuilt:
  - `omnia:identity`, `omnia:otel`, `omnia:plugins`, `omnia:vault`,
    `omnia:vcs`, and `omnia:websocket` keep `0.1.0`, so the host refuses
    the guest for a mismatched type.
  - `omnia:model` is `0.1.0`, where 0.37 shipped `0.2.0`, so the host
    refuses the guest for an import it does not serve.
- Native builds of `omnia-sdk`, and of `omnia-test` with the `guest`
  feature, link no `wasmtime`. `omnia-wasi-docstore` is a `wasm32`-only
  dependency of the SDK, and `MemoryDocs` keeps its own in-memory store.
- `Filter::on_date` accepts only a zero-padded `YYYY-MM-DD` date, so
  `2024-1-5` is an error. The error is an `omnia_sdk::Error` with the code
  `invalid`, which was an `anyhow::Error`.
- An `omnia_sdk::vcs::Error`, `plugins::Error`, or `model::Error` under
  `anyhow` context converts into `omnia_sdk::Error` with the code and
  status it has unwrapped, which was a `server_error`.
- A `model::Error` converts into `omnia_sdk::Error` under the code its
  `code()` names, as a `vcs::Error` or `plugins::Error` does:
  - `invalid-request` and `budget-exhausted` on a `BadRequest`, which were
    `bad_request`.
  - `unavailable` and `backend` on a `BadGateway`. A `backend` failure was
    `bad_gateway`.
  - `not-found`, `denied`, and `unsupported` on a `ServerError`.
- An `omnia_sdk::Error` under `anyhow` context adds its description, not
  its display, to the context. `bad_request!("no name")` under the
  context `saving` describes itself as `saving: no name`, which was
  `saving: code: bad_request, description: no name`.
- `omnia_sdk::Error` implements `PartialEq` and `Eq`.
- `omnia_sdk::Error::code()` and `description()` answer `&str`, which was
  a cloned `String`.
- A messaging failure is an `omnia_sdk::Error`, so `Router::handle`
  answers `omnia_sdk::Result<()>`. Its code names the failure:
  - `missing-topic`, a `BadRequest`, is a delivery with no topic, which
    was `DeliveryError::MissingTopic`.
  - `unhandled-topic`, a `NotFound`, is a topic no route takes, which was
    `DeliveryError::UnhandledTopic`.
  - `undecodable`, a `BadRequest`, is a payload that did not decode,
    whatever error a decoder refused it with. A broker drops it.
  - A handler's error takes the variant and code of the first
    `omnia_sdk::Error`, `vcs::Error`, `plugins::Error`, or `model::Error`
    in its source chain, and is otherwise a `ServerError`. Its description
    is the whole chain. A broker that redelivers delivers it again unless
    it is a `BadRequest`.

  Over `omnia:messaging`, a failure settles the delivery with the error's
  display as its reason. A `BadRequest` rejects it, and any other failure
  releases it. A match on a `DeliveryError` variant becomes a match on the
  code.
- A subcommand reads its handler's error by the messaging rule above, so
  `Command::call` takes any handler, where it took one whose error
  converted into `Failure`. `Command::hints` and `Router::hints` consult
  their fn for every failure.
- `omnia_sdk::api::Metadata` is `#[non_exhaustive]`. Code outside the SDK
  builds one with `Metadata::default()` and assigns its fields, or with
  `from_lookup`, `from_headers`, `from_env`, or `minted`.
- A request id minted natively draws 128 bits from the operating system's
  CSPRNG, as one minted in a guest draws from `wasi:random`.
- `SqlDefault` binds each `$n` placeholder to the `n`th parameter wherever
  it appears, so `SET name = $2 WHERE id = $1` no longer binds its values
  swapped. A `?` or `?n` placeholder binds as before, and any other named
  placeholder fails.

- `omnia_sdk::api::messaging::{consume, consume_with, Consume, Routes}`. A

- `omnia::wasi_view!`, the `Wasi*CtxView` types it generated, and the

- `omnia_wasi_docstore::document_store` and the SDK-typed

- The `omnia:docstore` `filter` resource, `query-options` record, and

- The `Default` impl of `ModelDefault`. `ModelDefault::connect_with` takes

  `Request::mcp_servers`, `omnia_sdk::model::McpGrant`, and `Tool::Mcp` in
  both crates. A tool the model calls is a function tool the guest runs.
  `omnia_sdk::mcp`, which serves MCP from a guest, stays.
- `Effort::Minimal`, in `omnia_wasi_model` and `omnia_sdk::model`.
  `Effort::Other("minimal")` reaches a backend that knows the level.
- `omnia_sdk::model::Error::ToolFailed`. A tool's failure text reaches the
  model, and a result over the host's size cap is `InvalidRequest`.
- The `sql` and `docstore` features of `omnia-sdk`. `SqlDb` and
  `DocumentDb` are always built.
- `omnia_test::guest::Namespaced`. A test addresses another bucket or
  locker with `Memory::named`.
- `omnia_test::guest::MemoryDocs::backend`.
- `omnia_wasi_keyvalue::Cacheable` and the guest-side `cache` module, the
  envelope a value set with a TTL was stored in.
- `omnia_sdk::CasError`. A conflict is `omnia_sdk::keyvalue::Conflict`.
- `omnia_sdk::api::DecodeError` and its `api::http` re-export. A
  `topic_with` or `command_with` decoder answers `omnia_sdk::Result<I>`, so
  `DecodeError::new(message)` becomes `bad_request!("{message}")`.
- The host's `omnia_wasi_sql::DataType`, which is `omnia_wasi_sql::Value`,
  and `omnia_wasi_sql::Error::trace`.
- The `wasi:blobstore@0.2.0-draft` imports, whose values are `wasi:io@0.2`
  streams a WASI 0.3 runtime does not serve. With them go
  `omnia_wasi_blobstore::{blobstore, container, types}` on `wasm32`, and

- The `wasi:keyvalue@0.2.0-draft2` imports. With them go

  the raw `omnia:keyvalue` bindings.
- The `wasi:sql@0.2.0-draft` imports. With them go
  `omnia_wasi_sql::{readwrite, types, DataType, Field, Row}` on `wasm32`,
  and the host's `ConnectionProxy`, `Prepared`, and `Trace`. A guest

  `omnia_wasi_sql::omnia` holds the raw `omnia:sql` bindings.
- The `wasi:config@0.2.0-rc.1` imports, which wasmtime's
  `wasmtime-wasi-config` served. With them go `omnia_wasi_config::store` on

  `omnia:config` bindings.
