## 0.38.0

Released 2026-11-23

### Added

- `omnia-wasi-types` (`omnia:types@0.1.0`) and Omnia-owned WIT packages (`omnia:blobstore`, `omnia:config`, `omnia:docstore`, `omnia:keyvalue`, `omnia:messaging`, `omnia:sql`), published to `omnia.host` with `xtask` (`bindgen`, `wit-sync`, `wit-check`, `wit-verify`) ([#393](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/393), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#401](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/401), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406), [#408](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/408), [#409](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/409)).
- Committed `wit-bindgen` guest bindings and per-interface modules in `omnia-sdk` (`blobstore`, `config`, `docstore`, `http`, `identity`, `keyvalue`, `messaging`, `model`, `otel`, `plugins`, `sql`, `vault`, `vcs`, `websocket`, plus `exports::messaging` and `exports::websocket`) ([#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- Guest SDK ergonomics: `#[derive(Settings)]` and `#[derive(FromRow)]`, `http::RequestBuilder`, `sql::Executor` and `params!`, conditional writes (`compare_and_swap`, `set_json_if`, `update_json`), `mcp::Tool::of`, `Metadata::deadline`, and `ErrorKind::Conflict` (`Error::conflict`, `conflict!`) ([#395](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/395), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- `omnia_sdk::http::cache::HttpCache` under the default `http` feature for outbound HTTP response caching in an `omnia:keyvalue` bucket via `Cache-Control` and `If-None-Match` ([#405](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/405), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- Unified transport registration on `omnia_sdk::{Handlers, Router}` (`route`, `topic`, `topic_with`, `request`, `request_with`, `command`, `command_with`, `into_router`) and `command::Render` ([#395](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/395), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- Per-guest and dynamic-plugin grants via `omnia::Grants` (`env`, `mounts`, `interfaces`, `Grants::within`, `Grants::validate`, `Runtime::validate_grants`, `StoreCtx::grants`) in `[guest.grants]`, `[plugins.grants]`, and `runtime!`, with a `plugin-load` span ([#417](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/417), [#421](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/421)).
- Explicit host variable allowlists via `omnia::env::{pattern, matches, PatternError}`: `[env] inherit` (`Manifest::inherit`, `Env`), `runtime!` `env`, `--env` on `Command::Run`, `omnia_test::host::Deployment::inherit`, and `omnia_wasi_config::ConnectOptions` (`CONFIG_KEYS`) ([#413](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/413), [#415](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/415)).
- Runtime concurrency gate (`MAX_CONCURRENT_INVOCATIONS`, `RuntimeOptions::max_concurrent_invocations`, `Runtime::{slot, try_slot, invocations}`, `Slot`, `Invocations`), unified guest entry `Runtime::enter` (`StoreCtx::guest`, `StoreBase::guest`), and graceful stop `Runtime::{stop, stopped}` ([#394](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/394), [#410](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/410), [#420](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/420)).
- HTTP probe handling via `HTTP_HEALTH_PATH` and `omnia_wasi_http::ServerOptions` (`HttpHandler::with_options`), plus streaming bounds `HTTP_BODY_BUFFER_CHUNKS` (`body_buffer_chunks`), `HTTP_STREAM_TIMEOUT_MS` (`http_stream_timeout`), and `WASI_MAX_IO_SIZE` (`wasi_max_io_size`) ([#418](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/418), [#419](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/419), [#420](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/420)).
- Host derives and runtime options: `#[derive(FromEnv)]` and `omnia::env` (`list`, `millis`, `paths`), `#[derive(HostCtx)]` and `omnia::{Backends, HostView}`, `StoreCtx::{view, mounts, dispatcher, extensions, chain}`, `DeploymentBuilder::options`, and `RuntimeOptions::{validate, total_core_instances, total_memories, total_tables, total_stacks}` ([#396](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/396), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#411](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/411), [#412](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/412)).
- Resource-allowlist options and OpenTelemetry runtime metrics: `ConnectOptions` (`KEYVALUE_BUCKETS`, `SQL_DATABASES`, `VAULT_LOCKERS`, `DOCSTORE_COLLECTIONS`, `MODEL_NAMES`) and `StubOptions` (`IDENTITY_NAMES`), plus `omnia::telemetry::{record_pool, record_invocations, count_message, count_event, count_error}` ([#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#416](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/416), [#420](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/420)).
- Component-rung doubles and contract suites in `omnia-test`: `omnia_test::host::{MatchedHttp, ScriptedTables, Sink, FixedIdentity, send, deliver, precompiled}` and `omnia_test::contract::{state, sql, document, blob}` behind the `contract` feature ([#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406), [#433](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/433)).

### Changed

- **WASI 0.3 and toolchain:** guests target `wasm32-wasip3` (`wasip3` 0.10, `wit-bindgen` 0.63, MSRV Rust 1.100), `omnia_test::build` compiles for `wasm32-wasip3`, the linker refuses WASI 0.2 imports with rebuild guidance, and suspending raw exports require `async func` ([#394](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/394)).
- **Wasmtime and SQL validation:** upgraded `wasmtime` and `wasmtime_wasi` to **51.0.0** (rebuild precompiled artifacts), and `omnia:sql` validates temporal values with `jiff` ([#401](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/401), [#414](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/414)).
- **Guest SDK (`omnia-sdk`):** handlers are stateless over direct WIT bindings with provider-free `Context`, `Handler`, `Handlers`, and `Router` at the crate root, and `http::export!`, `messaging::export!`, and `command::export!` take the router fn alone. `Error` is a struct over `ErrorKind` (`Error::kind`) implementing `IntoResponse` and converting from `omnia:types` `Error` and typed outcomes (`model::Exhausted`, `plugins::Conflict`, `vcs::{Occupied, Diverged, Unmerged}`), `McpServer::{call_tool, read_resource}` and `mcp::handle_message` are `async`, and `omnia-wasi-*` plus `omnia-plugin` are host-only crates ([#395](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/395), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- **WIT interfaces and default backends:** `omnia:identity`, `omnia:model`, `omnia:otel`, `omnia:plugins`, `omnia:vault`, `omnia:vcs`, and `omnia:websocket` are rewritten at `0.1.0`. Hosts report `omnia_wasi_types::Error` (except `omnia_wasi_vcs::Error`, which adds `Denied`) alongside typed outcomes (`omnia_wasi_model::Exhausted`, `omnia::LoadConflict`, `omnia_wasi_messaging::HandleError`, `Request::complete_checked` `Candidate`), default backends enforce declared resource names (`not-found` when undeclared), `SqlDefault` supports transactions and positional numbered parameter binding, and `KeyValueDefault` stores counters as decimal text ([#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- **Environment, config, and grants:** guests see only `RUST_LOG`, `NO_COLOR`, and `[env] inherit` or `--env` matches by default, and `ConfigDefault` serves nothing unless `CONFIG_KEYS` or `ConnectOptions::keys` names it. `Runtime::{register, admit}`, `Guest::local`, `StoreConfig`, and `Plugins::{install, load}` enforce `Grants`, returning `GuestError::Denied` on ungranted imports, mounts, or mismatched re-registration ([#413](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/413), [#415](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/415), [#417](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/417), [#421](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/421)).
- **Host traits, options, and CLI:** `Host`, `Deployment`, `Wiring`, `Registry`, `Guest`, `LinkSeam`, and `StoreFactory` parameterise over the `Backends` bundle (`omnia::Connect`, `omnia::ConnectAll`, `Host::run`, `KeyValueClient` / `BlobStoreClient` / `ModelClient` client traits, `Endpoint`, `WasiBlobStore`, `BlobStoreDefault`, `WasiPluginsView`, `omnia_wasi_http::HttpBorrow`). `omnia::{Cli, Command, Parser}` and `Command::Compile` build unconditionally, `RuntimeOptions` and `ConnectOptions` derive `FromEnv`, and pool defaults scale with `POOL_MAX_INSTANCES` ([#393](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/393), [#396](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/396), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406), [#411](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/411), [#412](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/412)).
- **HTTP server and concurrency gate:** `WasiHttp` serves HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2 on one listener, drains connections on the first `SIGTERM` or Ctrl-C signal (exiting on the second), answers `400` on malformed `Host`, `:authority`, or `Forwarded` targets, fails client streams on mid-body guest traps or `HTTP_STREAM_TIMEOUT_MS`, and answers `503` (`Retry-After`) when `MAX_CONCURRENT_INVOCATIONS` is full while messaging and command runs queue ([#394](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/394), [#418](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/418), [#420](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/420)).
- **Runtime telemetry:** pool, gate, and trigger metrics export directly as OpenTelemetry instruments under scope `omnia` (`invocations_in_flight`, `invocations_waiting`, `invocations_refused`, `processing_errors`) regardless of `RUST_LOG`, including gate occupancy when `POOLING=false` ([#416](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/416), [#420](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/420)).

### Removed

- `omnia-sdk` capability traits and WASI handles (`BlobStore`, `BlobStoreExt`, `Broadcast`, `Config`, `DocumentStore`, `HttpRequest`, `Identity`, `Model`, `Plugins`, `Publish`, `StateStore`, `TableStore`, `Vcs`, `CasError`, `WasiModel`, `WasiPlugins`, `WasiVcs`, `omnia_sdk::Message`, `omnia_sdk::document_store`), the `sql` and `docstore` features on `omnia-sdk`, and hidden `omnia_sdk` crate re-exports ([#393](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/393), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404)).
- `omnia_sdk::api` (`Client`, `DecodeError`, `Format`, `Encoded`, `HttpError`, `HttpResult`, `RawRequest`, `MethodFilter`, `Consume`, `DeliveryError`, `Routes`, `Failure::text`, `execute_wasi`) and `omnia_sdk::command!` ([#395](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/395), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404)).
- Model MCP grants and per-capability SDK errors (`omnia_wasi_model::{Mcp, Reply}`, `omnia_sdk::model::{McpGrant, Effort::Minimal, Error, Error::ToolFailed}`, `omnia_sdk::vcs::Error`, `omnia_sdk::plugins::Error`) and `omnia_wasi_keyvalue::cache` (`Cache`, `Cacheable`) ([#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404)).
- WASI 0.2 and draft WIT packages (`wasi:blobstore@0.2.0-draft`, `wasi:config@0.2.0-rc.1`, `wasi:docstore@0.1.0`, `wasi:keyvalue@0.2.0-draft2`, `wasi:messaging@0.2.0-draft`, `wasi:sql@0.2.0-draft`), `wasmtime_wasi_config`, `ConfigDefault::config_vars`, and all `wasm32` guest bindings on `omnia-wasi-*` crates ([#393](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/393), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#401](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/401), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404)).
- `omnia-test` `guest` feature and `omnia_test::guest` (`Provider`, `Memory`, `MemoryDocs`, `Namespaced`, `Scripted`, `ScriptedLoader`, `MatchedHttp`, `MapConfig`, `FixedIdentity`, `Sink`, `ScriptedTables`) ([#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#404](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/404)).
- Legacy host traits, macros, and proxy types: `omnia::{Backend, Server, StoreView, HasMounts, HasDispatcher, HasExtensions, HasChain, HasLimits, HttpBorrow, HttpCtx, LinkStore, serve_links, host_error, wasi_view, WasiPluginsCtxView, pastey}`, `omnia_wasi_http::WasiHttpView`, `WasiBlobstore`, `BlobstoreDefault`, `WasiKeyValueCtx` and `WasiKeyValueCtxView` (and sibling host context types), `omnia_wasi_docstore::{document_store, FilterTree, ScalarValue, ComparisonOp, FilterProxy, QueryOptions}`, `omnia_wasi_sql::{DataType, ConnectionProxy}`, `omnia_wasi_blobstore::{ContainerProxy, IncomingValue, OutgoingValue, StreamObjectNames}`, `omnia_wasi_keyvalue::{BucketProxy, Cas}`, `omnia_wasi_messaging::RequestOptions`, and `Default` on `DocStoreDefault` and `ModelDefault` ([#393](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/393), [#396](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/396), [#397](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/397), [#400](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/400), [#401](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/401), [#403](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/403), [#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406)).
- `Runtime::{store, store_in, build_store, instantiate, store_factory}`, and the `fromenv` dependency and builder API (`from_env`, `requirements`) ([#410](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/410), [#411](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/411)).
- Cargo features `cli`, `mpk`, and `gc` on `omnia`, `mpk` and `gc` on `omnia-core`, and `jit` on `omnia-cli`, `RuntimeOptions::{pool_total_gc_heaps, pool_memory_protection_keys, pool_max_memory_protection_keys}` (`POOL_TOTAL_GC_HEAPS`, `POOL_MEMORY_PROTECTION_KEYS`, `POOL_MAX_MEMORY_PROTECTION_KEYS`), and exported `pool_unused_warm_memories` / `pool_unused_memory_bytes_resident` metrics ([#406](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/406), [#416](https://github.com/augentic/omnia/pull/416)).
